# OctoAcme — Project Management Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle of Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective. Work is organized into small, shippable increments tracked on a project board (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done). The Pull Request workflow emphasizes small PRs, clear acceptance criteria, and CI checks (tests, linting, and security scans) before requesting review. Planning artifacts — including a Project One-pager, prioritized backlog items, release plan, and risk register — are produced early and updated during execution.

Clear roles and responsibilities provide ownership and decision paths: Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize the backlog; Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communications; Developers implement and test features; QA validates acceptance criteria and test plans. The repository contains persona templates and communication expectations to help teams align on what each role contributes across standups, PRs, and status updates.

Communication is regular and structured: short daily standups for progress and blockers, weekly delivery syncs for progress and risk discussion, demos at the end of sprints or milestones, and monthly stakeholder updates. Risk management uses a simple risk register (ID, description, impact/likelihood, owner, mitigation, status) and clear escalation paths (team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor) for fast resolution. Incident and weekly status templates are used to keep stakeholders informed.

Quality assurance is integrated across the lifecycle: unit and integration tests for new logic, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows before release, CI-based security scanning, and manual QA for acceptance when required. Releases follow a checklist (pre-release verification, rollback plan, staged deployments and smoke tests) and retrospectives capture action items that are tracked back into the backlog to drive continuous improvement.
